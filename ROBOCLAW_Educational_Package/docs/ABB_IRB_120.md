# ABB IRB 120 (IRB 120-3/0.6)

Compact 6-axis multipurpose industrial robot.

## Key Specifications (manufacturer data)
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Reach | 580 mm |
| Payload | 3 kg (up to 4 kg with vertical wrist) |
| Arm load (upper arm) | 0.3 kg |
| Pose repeatability | 0.01–0.02 mm |
| Number of axes | 6 |
| Robot weight | 25 kg |
| Base footprint | 180 mm × 180 mm |
| Height | approx. 700 mm |
| Mounting positions | Floor, wall, ceiling, any angle |
| Protection rating | IP30 (standard; cleanroom options available) |
| Ambient temperature (operation) | +5 °C to +45 °C |
| Controller | IRC5 Compact / IRC5 |
| Integrated interfaces | 10 signals + 4 air lines (max 5 bar) to wrist |

## Axis Motion Ranges & Speeds (typical)
- Axis 1: ±165° (250 °/s)
- Axis 2: ±110° (250 °/s)
- Axis 3: +70° / –110° (250 °/s)
- Axis 4: ±160° (320 °/s)
- Axis 5: ±120° (320 °/s)
- Axis 6: ±400° (420 °/s)

## Performance Example
1 kg pick-and-place cycle (25 × 300 × 25 mm): ~0.58 s

## Typical Applications
Packaging and tray insertion, electronics and small-part assembly, laboratory sample handling, medical-device manufacturing, food handling, and clean-room compatible processes.

The IRB 120 is valued for its very small footprint, high speed, and accuracy in tight production or laboratory cells.

# Educational Notes – Lab Context

These two robots represent the modern class of compact 6-axis industrial arms used for high-speed, high-precision tasks under ~1.1 m reach.

**Key learning themes**
- Payload vs. reach trade-offs and why rated payload is often lower than maximum
- Axis limits, singularities, and workspace shape
- Mounting flexibility (floor / wall / ceiling) and its effect on available workspace
- Repeatability requirements for assembly, inspection, and packaging
- Safety principles when working near industrial robots (fencing, collaborative modes, speed limits)

All numerical data are taken from official manufacturer product specifications and publicly available datasheets.

# Pre-Lab Reading Packet  
## Getting Ready for Your RoboClaw Lab

Read this short packet before your live lab session. It will help you understand the two robots you will control and the history behind industrial robots.

### The Two Robots You Will Use

**KUKA KR 10 R1100-2**  
- Reach: 1101 mm  
- Maximum payload: ~11 kg  
- Repeatability: ±0.02 mm  
- 6 axes  
- Very fast and precise — used in packaging, electronics assembly, and machine tending.

**ABB IRB 120**  
- Reach: 580 mm  
- Payload: 3 kg (up to 4 kg with wrist down)  
- Weight of the robot itself: only 25 kg  
- Extremely compact — often used in tight spaces, laboratories, and small-part handling.

### Quick History Snapshot
Industrial robots began in 1961 when the first Unimate arm started work at a General Motors factory, removing hot metal parts that were dangerous for people. Over the next decades, robots became electric, faster, more accurate, and much smaller. The robots in your lab are modern examples of that progress.

### What You Will Practice in the Live Lab
- Moving a real industrial robot arm
- Understanding axes and reach
- Seeing how precise these machines can be
- Basic control concepts that professional robot programmers use

Bring curiosity. You do not need to memorize everything — the live lab will make the ideas real.

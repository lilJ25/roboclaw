# ROBOCLAW Educational Games
Active learning activities that reinforce the live lab
Full-page printable versions included where noted

### 1. Axis Challenge (2–4 players)
Players take turns naming one axis (A1–A6) and describing what that joint typically does. Correct answers score a point. Bonus point for correctly stating a typical motion range from the lab robots.

### 2. Reach vs Payload Trade-off
Give students two imaginary tasks (example: “pick a 2 kg part from 900 mm away” and “pick a 9 kg part from 400 mm away”). They must choose which of the two lab robots is better suited and explain why using reach and payload data.

### 3. True / False Speed Round
Read statements from the Factoid List or history. Students stand for true, sit for false. Fastest correct responders score points.

### 4. Design a Cell (Paper Activity)
Students sketch a simple work cell that uses either the KUKA or ABB robot. They must label: robot base, reach zone, safety fence or scanner, and the part being handled.

### 5. Sequence the History
Print the major milestones (Unimate 1961, FAMULUS 1973, IRB 6 1974, IRB 120 2009, etc.). Students arrange them in correct chronological order.

### 6. Vocabulary Relay
Teams race to match terms (axis, reach, payload, repeatability, end effector, controller) to their definitions. First correct complete set wins.

### 7. One-Axis Only Challenge
In discussion or on paper: describe how you would move the robot to a point using only one axis at a time. Forces clear thinking about joint roles.

### 8. Safety Scan
Students list five safety habits that apply both in the ROBOCLAW Lab and on a real factory floor. Share and discuss.

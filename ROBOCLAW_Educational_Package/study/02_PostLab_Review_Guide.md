# Post-Lab Review Guide  
## After Your Live RoboClaw Session

Use this guide after you finish the live lab. It helps lock in what you just experienced.

### Reflection Questions
1. Which robot felt faster or more precise to you? Why do you think that is?
2. What surprised you most about controlling a real industrial robot?
3. How is moving a robot different from moving a character in a video game?
4. Why do you think factories use robots instead of only human workers for certain jobs?

### Key Ideas to Remember
- **Reach** is how far the robot can stretch.
- **Payload** is how much weight it can safely carry.
- **Repeatability** means the robot can return to almost exactly the same position every time.
- Industrial robots are designed for speed, accuracy, and safety in factories and labs.
